PURPOSE
  The bearpaw-export -> BC125AT SS -> re-save round-trip earlier today showed
  Bearpaw writes `AUTO` where Uniden writes `Auto`, on all 500 rows. The tool
  accepted our file, but every channel on this unit is AUTO with tone Off, so
  that result cannot distinguish the tool PARSING our value from the tool
  DISCARDING it and writing its own default. Both give identical output on
  uniform data.

  This probe creates rows that disagree with their neighbours.

BANK 9 (401-450) is the designated scratch bank. 27 of its 50 slots are
programmed; 428-450 are empty. The probe writes ONLY empty slots, so nothing
programmed was touched and restore is just clearing them back.

  Two failures on the way, both mine, both worth recording:

    tone_dcs_code=23   -> 400 {"error":"tone_invalid"}
        The field is the RAW PROTOCOL CODE (128-231), not the Motorola number.
        DCS 023 is 128. See protocol/tones.rs::dcs_code_to_number.

    body without `bank` -> 422 Unprocessable Entity
        ChannelData.bank has no #[serde(default)], so it is required on the
        PUT body. 422 arrives before any readable validation message.
